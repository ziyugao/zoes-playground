Versor

From the Latin versari, to turn. A quaternion of norm one: Hamilton’s name for a pure rotation.

q = cos a + r sin a,  r² = −1

 
 

Each ring turns by its own versor; the innermost carries their product, which is still a versor. Hamilton drew it as an arc of length a on the great circle at right angles to r. The rotation it performs, v ↦ qvq⁻¹, is twice that arc.
